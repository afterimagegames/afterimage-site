Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Who we are

Hindsight is a puzzle game published by Afterimage Games. Contact: afterimagegames@gmail.com.

What data the game collects

None. Hindsight does not ask you to create an account and does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else. The game works offline.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, your best scores, your sound setting, your chosen colour palette and mode, and whether you have unlocked the full game. This information is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Purchases

If the game offers an optional one-time purchase, it is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google and are never visible to us or to the game. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on it. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
